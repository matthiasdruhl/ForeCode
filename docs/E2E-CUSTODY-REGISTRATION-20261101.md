## The exact lifecycle and schedule constraint

`META_LIMITS.artifactTtlMs` is 1,800,000 ms. The trusted service retains proposals and issued hypotheses only in memory. The usable interval ends at the earliest of parent proposal acceptance + 30 minutes, issued hypothesis + 30 minutes, registration expiry, or any required original expiry. Context replacement, intent revision, a newer accepted hypothesis, process replacement, revocation or changed original bytes can end eligibility sooner. No original file can restore a retired UUID.

`service.register(registrationId, hypothesisId)` checks issuance before and after authenticated asynchronous custody resolution, and consumes both identifiers once. `service.resolveRegistered(...)` requires prior enrollment and rechecks live issuance and custody before and after each resolution. Registered measurement uses that fresh resolver on both complete passes. Optional interpretation rechecks issuance and the authenticated disclosure adapter before inference and again after inference. A stale process, cached parsed object or persisted historical record cannot substitute for these calls.

The stock Next singleton exposes HTTP Scientist calls but no operator enrollment route or hot provisioning. Restarting after editing its null resolver loses issuance. The eligible route is a separately reviewed private, single-process source composition staged **before generation**, whose trusted continuation can supply the freshly held exact binding pins without restarting the issued service. No such operational composition was demonstrated here.

For the historical 600-second-per-arm profile, maximum sequential downstream time alone is 1,200 seconds. At most 600 seconds remain after parent acceptance for hypothesis/challenge latency, binding, source preparation/review, baseline grading, admissions, sealing, measurements, original custody, disclosure and any optional interpretation. Actual provider latency further reduces the window; the transport has a 30-second call timeout and a ten-second minimum call interval. Preparation and custody durations have no demonstrated bound. A timed genuine rehearsal or a credible complete schedule is absent. Consequently this audit does **not** certify that a full 600-second pair plus all required work fits. Registering quickly alone is insufficient.

Retain the existing TTL. If a complete timetable cannot fit, stop dependent registered measurements/interpretation; a durable issuance design would require separate technical/security review and contract-compatible implementation. Do not reissue a new UUID onto already prepared work, backdate an original, or refresh an old generation to claim a fresh study window.
